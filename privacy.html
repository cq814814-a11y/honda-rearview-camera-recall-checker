Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 26, 2026.

Recall Camera Check is a free informational tool. The checker runs in your browser and requests public recall records from the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) API.

VIN handling

The optional VIN field is only used to create a link to the official NHTSA recall lookup page. This site does not store your VIN and does not send it to our server.

Analytics and advertising

This starter version does not include analytics or advertising scripts. If ads or analytics are added later, this policy should be updated to describe those services.

External links

This site links to NHTSA.gov and other official resources. Those external websites have their own privacy practices.
