Recent camera-related recalls

Why this checker exists

26V306000

2024-2025 Honda Prologue and 2024 Acura ZDX

NHTSA records describe a rearview camera screen that may display a distorted or blank image. Dealers are expected to replace the rearview camera free of charge.

26V112000

2024 Honda Prologue and 2024 Acura ZDX

NHTSA records describe a software issue that may cause the instrument panel display to fail and the rearview camera to show a blank screen in reverse.

23V431000

2018-2023 Odyssey, 2019-2022 Pilot, 2019-2023 Passport

NHTSA records describe a connector issue where the rearview camera image may not appear on the display.

Questions

Honda rearview camera recall FAQ

Can this page tell me if my exact Honda has an open recall?

It can show recall matches for a model year, make, and model. To confirm the exact status of one vehicle, enter your VIN on the official NHTSA recall page or contact a dealer.

Is a rearview camera recall repaired for free?

Safety recall remedies are generally performed free of charge by dealers. Always read the remedy text in the NHTSA record and confirm with your dealer before scheduling service.

Why do some results show Acura?

Acura is part of American Honda Motor Co. Some NHTSA campaigns include both Honda and Acura vehicles, especially the Prologue and ZDX camera-related recalls.

Does this site store my VIN?

No. The optional VIN field only builds a direct NHTSA lookup link in your browser. It is not sent to this website or stored.
